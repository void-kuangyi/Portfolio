<TextLayout>
  <b>Introduction</b>
  <Text>
    I collaborated with Rhinannin and together with their band consisting of
    Frank Wintermans (drums), Timon Koomen (guitar) and Jornt Duyx (keyboards,
    bass, guitar), shown at her album release at{" "}
    <a href="https://www.effenaar.nl/agenda/rhiannin-0">Effenaar</a> during
    Dutch Design Week 2024. <br /> <br />
    The project, Bodylanguage, also the name of the album, explored how to transform
    body language into an immersive visual and audio experience. The innovative installation
    responds visually and audibly to her movements and emotions, inviting the audience
    to experience her multidisciplinary art with all their senses. After the completion
    of the project, the LED installation has been travelling with her and the band,
    showed at <a href="https://paradoxtilburg.nl/">Paradox Tilburg</a>, <a href="https://introinsitu.nl/">
      Intro in Situ
    </a> and more in the future.
  </Text>
  <b>Technology</b>
  <Text>
    I created 13 unique visuals for her songs, turning her paintings,
    silhouettes, and movements into interactive art, using live body tracking
    with Orbbec camera in <a href="https://derivative.ca/">TouchDesigner</a>. I
    also turned visuals into audio reactive, enabled by acoustic drum triggers.
    <br /> <br />I worked with Eva van der Born on developing a human theremin, designed
    with leap motion hand tracking and Abelton, that translates Rhiannin’s hand gestures
    into a musical narrative—allowing her to manipulate her voice and conduct the
    sound simultaneously.
  </Text>
</TextLayout>
